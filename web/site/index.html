Carrot Crawler

One tiny rogue-like, three ways to play it. You are @. Collect gold, dodge the carrots, descend past floor 99 to win. Teleporting costs 3 gold and may save your life.

Full 3D First-person dungeon crawler with real 3D assets, music, and mouse look. Made with Godot. Terminal raycaster The same dungeon rendered Wolfenstein-style in a text terminal, with mouse look. Written in C with curses. The original The untouched, obfuscated, award-winning IOCCC 2025 entry. Turn-based, top-down, 4 KB of C.
